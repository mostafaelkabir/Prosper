Stolen Glance · launch reveal · 2.8 s
The look you didn't mean to take, locked away.
The idea

An eye that lowers its gaze, and a pupil that turns into a lock.

The name comes from a verse about the glance you steal when you think no one sees. The mark tells that story in five beats: the eye opens, the glance slips sideways, the lid comes down, and when it opens again the pupil is a keyhole. The lids are drawn like a pen stroke, thick in the middle and sharp at the corners. At rest the upper lid sits low and the iris looks a little down.

“He knows the stolen glance of the eyes.”Ghafir 40:19
The mark

Four surfaces, one shape

Aurora gradient on night and day, and single-colour versions for print, stamps and the shield screen. The keyhole is cut out, so the background always shows through it.

App icon

Built to read at 29 points

The eye fills 70% of the tile, so the keyhole is still a keyhole on Settings rows and Spotlight. A soft violet glow sits behind the iris, the same lift the current icon uses.

Lockups

With the name

The wordmark is Sora Semibold with tight tracking. Keep clear space of one keyhole height around the lockup, and never set the name without the eye in a place where the eye would fit.

Motion spec

Every beat, timed

Each beat is built from a named 60fps shot. Under Reduce Motion the whole reveal becomes a 300 ms fade to the resting mark. It plays once on launch and never loops.
